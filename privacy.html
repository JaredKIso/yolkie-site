Privacy

What we keep, and why.

Yolkie is a pre-launch concept, so we keep very little. Here’s all of it, in plain words. Last updated 26 October 2026.

The short version

  • The demo saves everything on your device. We never see it.
  • We only keep what you send us: a waitlist email, feedback, or an account.
  • No ads, no tracking scripts, no selling your data. Ever.
  • You can delete your account yourself, any time, from your account page.

On your device

Your meals, shopping list, cards, stickers, progress and settings in the demo live in your browser’s storage on this device. They aren’t sent anywhere. Clear them in the demo’s settings, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

The site also remembers your light or dark choice, and, if you sign in, your login, so you stay signed in. The site doesn’t set cookies itself.

What you send us

The waitlist

Your email, your answer to “What would make Yolkie a must-have?”, and which page you joined from. We use your email only to tell you about Yolkie.

Feedback

Your rating and answers, any screenshots or videos you add, which page you came from and the site’s version. Your email too, if you give it, so we can reply. Please don’t include personal health details like your own allergies or conditions.

An account

Your email, the name you choose, how you sign in, when you joined, and your badges. If you use a password, it’s stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it, us included. If you sign in with GitHub, Google or Apple, they tell us your email and name. We don’t get your password there or anything else from your account. With Apple, you can hide your real email.

Who helps us run it

  • Supabase stores the waitlist, feedback, screenshots and accounts, and handles signing in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it may log basic visit details such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site’s fonts and icons, so your browser asks Google’s servers for them.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, when switched on, checks that the email sign-in form is used by a person, not a bot. It looks at signals from your browser to do that.
  • GitHub, Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with one of them.

Who can see it

Only the small Yolkie team, on a private admin page. The website can add a waitlist email or a feedback note, but it can’t read them back, so nobody else can see what you sent. Your account details are visible to you and to the team.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: use “Delete my account” on your account page. Your login, name and badges are erased straight away.
  • The waitlist or feedback: these aren’t tied to an account. To have them removed, send a note on the feedback page with the email you used and ask. We’ll remove them and reply.

We keep the waitlist and feedback while they help us build Yolkie, and remove them when they don’t, or when you ask.
