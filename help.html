Quick start
  1. Pick the meal: breakfast, lunch, dinner or a snack.
  2. Tap your energy (Wiped, Okay or Good) and how much time you have.
  3. Yolkie suggests one meal. Tap “Let’s make this”, tick what you have, then “Start cooking”.
  4. Follow one step at a time until it says “Time to eat”.
Try it
02

Picking a meal
  1. Choose the meal type, your energy and your time. Lower energy means simpler recipes, and 60+ opens up the long, slow ones.
  2. “What do you already have?” is optional: tap up to three things and Yolkie leans toward meals that use them.
  3. On a Wiped day, or with 15 minutes, Yolkie only suggests meals made from what you picked plus pantry basics like oil, butter, salt, flour, sugar and soy sauce. Nothing to buy. The suggestion says “Only using what you picked”: tap it to also see meals that need one more thing, shown with a “Needs” tag. Tap it again to switch back.
  4. Whenever you’ve picked something, each suggestion lists what it still needs that you didn’t pick, leaving out pantry basics. Where there’s an easy swap you’ll see “Use …”, like garlic powder for garlic, and “Skip it” if the dish works without it. Either one ticks that item off on the next step and keeps it off your shopping list. Changed your mind? Tap Undo.
  5. Some recipes have a “Pro tip” with an extra, like toast with your eggs. It’s only if you have it: it’s never on the shopping list, and it doesn’t change what Yolkie picks.
  6. If you told Yolkie what you had in the last few days, “Still got them?” asks first. It’s only a guess: say yes, or tap anything that’s gone.
  7. “Who’s eating?” starts as just you, every meal. Tap anyone else who’s eating and the picks work for all of you.
  8. Want to choose yourself? “Find by ingredient” lists every recipe that has everything you add, like chicken and rice, for everyone eating. Tap one to make it the pick. Open the finder.
  9. A “Recipe by …” chip means the recipe is based on a creator’s recipe. It links to their original page. The usage page explains how we use and credit them.

03

Diets and restrictions
  1. Turn on diets (vegan, keto, gluten-free and more) and allergies in Preferences, behind the gear.
  2. The diet chip on the pick screen changes your diet for “Just this meal” or “Make it my default”.
  3. Each person in the house has their own diets, allergies and dislikes. Dislikes only move a recipe down; allergies always filter it out for anyone eating.
  4. “Keep out of the house” filters an allergen out of every suggestion, even when that person isn’t eating.

infoWe filter recipes, but always check labels.

04

Shopping list
  1. After you pick a meal, tap “Got it” or “Need it” for each ingredient.
  2. Everything you need lands on the list, sorted by store section (produce, dairy, pantry and so on).
  3. Out of a spice? Tap it for an easy swap, or add it to the list. Tick items off as you shop, and Yolkie remembers them for a few days.
Try it
05

Cook mode
  1. Set the servings first: the amounts scale for you.
  2. Step zero is one tiny, physical thing, like putting a pan on the stove. Momentum does the rest.
  3. The tracker at the top shows where you are: Prep, Cook, Finish, Plate. Under it, Yolkie estimates when you'll eat (“Dinner at 8:12”) and updates it as you go.
  4. The “Now” card shows one step. Timers run side by side as rings that fill up, so the rice and the sauce finish together. A soft chime plays when one ends; turn it off under Sounds in Preferences.
  5. Small labels under each step show how much of each ingredient that step needs, scaled to your servings, so you can measure as you go or eyeball it.
  6. While you wait, Yolkie suggests a quick tidy, so there are fewer dishes later.

06

Nutrition and water
  1. Calories, protein and the nutrition panel are rough estimates per serving. “Hide nutrition” in Preferences hides every number.
  2. The water tracker is off until you turn it on in Preferences. Then its card is on Me: tap “+1” when you drink, and “−” to undo.
  3. Each tap waters the plant in Yolkie’s Kitchen. Reach your goal and it blooms for the rest of the day. It starts fresh every morning, with no streaks.
  4. Pick the unit, your bottle size and your goal in Preferences, or turn the tracker off. Water reminders are a preview here: the real ones will come from the app.

infoEveryone’s needs are different. If a doctor has given you a fluid limit, follow that.

07

Progress
  1. Me shows how many meals you’ve cooked at home (with “about $X saved” on takeout: meals × your usual takeout cost, an estimate), fridge rescues and dishes dodged.
  2. The passport stamps every new cuisine and recipe.
  3. Set a weekly goal, and start a themed challenge if you like. One at a time, no deadline.
  4. There are no streaks and no guilt: a missed week just starts fresh.

08

Yolkie’s Closet and Yolkie’s Kitchen
  1. Cooking unlocks outfits in Yolkie’s Closet (in Me) and decor in the Kitchen. The Kitchen is behind the Extras card on Me.
  2. In the Kitchen, tap the floor to walk Yolkie around, and tap anyone to say hi.
  3. Everyone else at home cooks, washes up, raids the fridge, eats, chats and makes coffee on their own. “Who’s home” chooses who’s there.
  4. Turn on Night for string lights and dancing. “Friends drop by” lets friends you’ve met visit now and then.
Try it
09

Collection
  1. Every recipe you finish drops its card into your binder, on the Cards tab. Cook it 5 times and it turns foil. Rarity comes from effort, never luck, and nothing is ever sold.
  2. Stickers, friends, the Pantry-dex and gifts are in Extras → Collection (the Extras card is on Me). Fun moments earn fridge stickers. Drag them onto the fridge, in the Collection or in the Kitchen. Cozy bowl comes with any soup in Soup Season (October to March).
  3. Cook with avocado, bread, berries or noodles, or make an egg brunch, to meet the friends. Every ingredient fills a Pantry-dex entry.
  4. Spare stickers can be gifted to someone in the house or turned into crumbs. Three crumbs pick any sticker you’re missing.

10

Favorites
  1. After a meal, everyone who ate taps 😕, 🙂 or 😍. Pass the phone.
  2. Recent meals in House (Extras → House) has three tabs: Recent, My favorites and House favorites.
  3. “Make again” jumps straight into cook mode. On a Wiped day, favorites come up as a “Comfort pick”.
  4. “Don’t suggest this again” hides a meal. Undo it any time in Preferences. There’s no “worst meals” list.
Try it
11

Household
  1. Everyone starts as a house of one. In Extras → House, add people, or tap “Try it as a house” to have Boyd and Scramble move in for the demo.
  2. In House, “Create household” makes an invite code (like YOLK-7KQ2), a link and a QR code. Codes last 7 days.
  3. Someone joins with the code and waits for the owner to tap Approve or Decline.
  4. “Add someone without the app” makes a guest profile you look after. When they get the app, “This is me” hands it over.
  5. Members, restrictions, the shopping list, votes, the house goal and favorites are shared. Personal stats, nutrition, water, check-in times and sticker books stay private. Leaving or removing someone never takes their stats.

Check-in reminders
  1. Set reminder times before breakfast, lunch or dinner in Preferences, plus quiet hours and “Not today”.
  2. “Preview the check-in” (in Preferences → More settings → Check-in reminders) shows the lock-screen notification you’d get. Tap your energy and the meal and shopping list are ready.
  3. In the real app, it arrives about an hour before the meal. The demo can only show a preview.

Your account

You don’t need an account to try the demo. An account is free, and right now it gives you:

  • Your own name on Yolkie, and an account page with your badges.
  • Badges: Yolkling for everyone with an account, Early adopter if you join before launch, and Tester if you help us test.
  • A way in as a tester. Testers hear about new things first. Join the waitlist or ask on the feedback page, using the email you signed up with, and your Tester badge appears when you’re accepted.
  • One sign-in for what’s coming next.

What’s coming

For now, your meals, cards and progress in the demo still save on this device only. Next, we’re working on saving them to your account, so they follow you between your phone and computer, and on real household linking between phones.

Signing in

Use GitHub, or an email and password. With an email, tap the link we send to confirm it before you sign in. Forgot your password? Tap “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page and we’ll email you a link to pick a new one.

Two-step sign-in

Turn it on from your account page with an authenticator app, like Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy. Then signing in asks for a 6-digit code from the app as well as your password or GitHub. Tick “Remember this device for 30 days” to skip the code on your own phone or computer. Picking a new password on Yolkie asks for a code too, even on a remembered device.

When you turn it on, you get ten backup codes. Save them somewhere safe: if you lose your phone, each one gets you in once instead of an app code. You can make a new set any time.

Deleting your account

On your account page, “Delete my account” erases your login, name and badges straight away. The privacy page says what we keep and how to ask us to remove a waitlist email or feedback.

For testers

What to try

  • Cook a meal start to finish, and watch a card flip in.
  • Add someone to “Who’s eating?” with an allergy, and see the picks change.
  • Log water to the goal, then visit the Kitchen to see the plant bloom.
  • Put a sticker on the fridge, and gift a spare.
  • Create a household code, join with it, and approve the new member.

Sample data

Preferences → “Load sample week” fills in a made-up few weeks: meals, cards, stickers, friends, ratings and more. It’s kept apart from your real stats. Clear it from the yellow banner or from Preferences.

Start over

Preferences → “Clear all demo data” (tap it twice) removes everything the demo saved in this browser.

Tell us how it went

The feedback form takes about two minutes. Only the star rating is required. Found a bug? Say what happened and add up to three screenshots or a short video. Got an idea? There’s a box for that too. Want to hear when Yolkie launches? Join the waitlist.

FAQ
Where’s my data?
Everything you do in the demo stays in this browser, on this device, and “Clear all demo data” removes it. What you send us (the waitlist, feedback, an account) is stored with Supabase, and the fonts load from Google. The privacy page lists all of it, and how to delete it.
Where does my feedback go?
Straight to the Yolkie team’s private database (we use Supabase). Only the team can read it. Screenshots go into a private folder with no public link. Your answers aren’t shared or sold, and an email you add is only used to reply to you or, if you’d like, to invite you to test new things. Please leave out personal health details.
Is one of your recipes in Yolkie?
Some recipes are based on creators’ published recipes, credited with a link back. If one is yours and you’d like it taken out, use the recipe removal form (or “Is this your recipe?” under the credit). We’ll review and remove it within 2 business days.
Is this the real app?
No. It’s a working demo of the app, made before launch, so you can try the ideas and tell us what you think.
Why doesn’t household linking sync between phones yet?
Real linking needs the demo to save to a server, not just your phone. Accounts are the first step, and they’re here now. Until linking arrives, the demo shows how it will work, all on one phone.
Where do the recipes come from?
Some are Yolkie’s own. Others are based on recipes creators share on their own sites, credited with a link on every one. The usage page has the details.
Are nutrition numbers exact?
No. They’re rough estimates per serving, from typical ingredients. Brands and portions vary.
Can Yolkie guarantee allergy safety?
No. Yolkie filters recipes by the allergies you set, but it can’t know every product. Always check labels.
