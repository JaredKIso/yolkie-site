Usage

Where the recipes come from.

Some recipes in the demo are Yolkie’s own. Others are based on recipes that creators share for free on their own sites. Here’s how we use them and how we credit them.

Yolkie’s own recipes

The first recipes in the demo were written for Yolkie. They have no source chip.

Recipes from creators

A recipe based on a creator’s recipe has a linkRecipe by … chip that links to the original page. Here’s what we take from it and what we leave out:

  • Kept as the creator made it: the ingredients and amounts, the servings, temperatures, the order of the steps and their timings. Recipes are made the way they are for a reason.
  • Written by Yolkie: the wording of each step, made shorter for cook mode and split so timers can run.
  • Not copied: photos, videos, the creator’s story, tips, ratings and reviews. Those stay on their site. For all of that, follow the link.

infoYolkie isn’t affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by these creators.

Diet, allergen and other tags

Yolkie works out the diet and allergen tags from each recipe’s ingredient list, whether or not the creator labels them. We watch for the hidden ones, like stock in an otherwise vegetarian dish, or gluten in soy sauce. When a recipe only fits a diet after a swap, it says so instead of getting the tag.

The tags are our reading of the ingredients, not the creator’s. Brands vary, so check the labels if an allergy is serious.

Nutrition

Where a creator publishes nutrition for a recipe, the demo uses their numbers per serving, rounded, and Yolkie fills any gap with its own estimate. Either way, they’re estimates.

For creators

Is one of your recipes in the demo? If you’d like the credit changed or the recipe taken out, tell us and we’ll do it. On the feedback form, choose “Neither, just general thoughts”, name the recipe under “Anything else?”, and add your email so we can reply. The star rating is the one required answer, so any rating is fine.
